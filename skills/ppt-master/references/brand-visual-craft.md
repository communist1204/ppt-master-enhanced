# Brand proposal and visual makeover craft

Load for brand proposals, product launches and explicitly requested makeovers.
Retain the chosen upstream route and all native object/image contracts. The aim
is a coherent authored visual identity, not a universal minimal or colorful style.

## Identity before page furniture

Resolve the actual setting and brand character before choosing color and type.
A quiet architecture proposal may call for restrained serif type, stone-colored
fields, controlled letterspacing and scenic light. A product launch may call for
large sans type, material contrast, a concentrated accent plane and a product
silhouette. These are examples, not mandatory palettes or page templates. Do not
carry a scientific blue/teal theme into every subject by default.

Treat text as part of composition: a deliberately large brand name, a compact
kicker, a headline with meaningful line breaks and a precise small caption can
create hierarchy without enclosing each unit in a card. Keep recurring type roles
stable. Use only fonts approved/installed on the delivery target, and review their
real PowerPoint rendering. Do not fake editable type by generating whole-page art.

## Image and composition

Choose the focal asset, its actual shape, its quiet area and the caption job before
placing it. Let an image establish the page's space through a full-bleed scene,
an asymmetric editorial crop, a product cutout crossing a native color plane, or
a measured diptych when that improves the page. A small decorative picture beside
a dense text list is not a substitute for the visual story requested by the user.
An honest text-led page is still valid when the image has no communication job.

Apply the content-to-expression decision in `enhanced-delivery.md` here. The
brand system governs treatment and continuity; the page's information need
determines what must be visible and how it is organized. Keep text legible through image selection,
placement and an intentional scrim; do not obscure the subject with heavy slabs.
A crop should read as intentional: avoid an isolated severed product silhouette
floating inside an unbounded background; keep the complete silhouette or give
the detail crop a deliberate edge. Transparent cutouts need clean edges when viewed over both the intended light and
dark fields. Intentional object/image overlap is valid, but text must not vanish
behind a product in the final deck. Mark conceptual or generated scenes honestly.

## Deck rhythm and makeover evaluation

Give each page a job: opening presence, explanation, concrete evidence or plan,
and closing recall. Vary scale, crop and density inside one identity rather than
repeating the same left-text/right-picture layout. Keep a deliberate quiet page
when the deck benefits from it. A dominant color plane or large numeral should
organize actual content; empty ornament is not a design improvement.

For a makeover, retain the user's wording/order/data when specified; compare
unchanged content at equal render size. The visible change should be explainable
in terms of hierarchy, image integration, composition, typography and rhythm.
Keep the best qualities of the original and do not weaken the baseline to make
the comparison dramatic. If a stronger visual pass is hand-directed beyond normal
automatic invocation, say so; do not imply one sample proves an upgraded Skill
will always outperform its parent. Disclose the actual added visual ideas at delivery.
